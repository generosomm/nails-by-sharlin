# Design direction

Pearl paper `#f5f3f7`, plum ink `#302333`, berry accent `#57334f`, muted orchid `#6b5f70`, soft line `#ddd5e0`, white `#ffffff`.

Use local Segoe UI with Helvetica/Arial fallback for compact, understated typography. Fluid headlines and restrained body copy echo the reference portfolio's hierarchy, while a light palette gives Sharlin her own identity.

Layout: left-aligned headline beside one sculptural, glossy nail fan; open service rows below; a soft inset contact area with a ready-to-edit SMS inquiry.

```
wordmark                           services / contact
headline + text CTA                glossy nail fan
                                   color selector
service menu                       prices
contact details                    SMS inquiry
footer                             Facebook search
```

Review: avoid a generic spa template with stock photos, invented reviews and a grid of sales cards. Concentrate the 3D treatment on the nail fan. Keep service rows plain and prices visible. The swatches explicitly represent inspiration rather than actual work. Respect reduced motion and support keyboard input.
